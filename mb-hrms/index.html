PREFERENCES

Settings

Demo settings and data handling.

Demonstration configuration

Accounts and data are stored in this browser only (local storage). Sign-in is client-side access control for demonstration, not a secure multi-user HR database. Do not enter confidential employee information.

SGI formula: weighted sum of six 0–100 training-need indicators. Demo weights are equal unless edited; validate weights and thresholds before operational use.
